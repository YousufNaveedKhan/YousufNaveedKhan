## ⚡ About Me

> *“Software is only as good as the business problem it actually solves.”*

I write software and I teach it — bridging the gap between academic theory and real-world execution. My core stack revolves around **MERN** and **.NET/C#**. As Senior Faculty at Aptech Gulshan 2 (my alma mater), I focus on teaching *why* a technology exists, *how* it works under the hood, and *where* it is deployed in production.

Beyond the classroom, I run **GFT Solutions**, handling day-to-day operations and building production-grade software where real-world constraints (inventory, reconciliation, and scale) matter. 

*On a personal note, being a Hafiz-e-Quran and a 4th-year Dars-e-Nizami student has heavily shaped my discipline, deep-work capacity, and long-term commitment to mastery.*
